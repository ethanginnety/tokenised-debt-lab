Why prices fall when yields rise

A bond's payments are fixed. Its price is what those payments are worth today, discounted at current rates. Higher rates shrink every future payment's value, and the further away a payment is, the more it shrinks. That is why the 100-year bond moves so much more than the 3-year one.

Duration in one line

Modified duration is the approximate % price change for a 1 percentage point yield move. A duration of 14 means a 1 point rise in yield costs roughly 14% of the price. Convexity is the correction for the fact that this relationship curves.

Clean and dirty price

The dirty price is what a buyer actually pays: the bond's value including interest built up since the last coupon. The clean price strips that accrued interest out, so it doesn't jump on coupon dates. Bonds are quoted clean.
